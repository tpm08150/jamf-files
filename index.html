Download the Mac app

Keeps a folder on this machine matching the event's, and tells you when a file changes. Apple Silicon Macs.

⚠️ macOS will refuse it the first time

A downloaded app is quarantined, and this one is not signed with a paid Apple certificate — so you get “Apple could not verify Event Files is free of malware”. Do not press Move to Trash. Press Done, then either:

  1. System Settings → Privacy & Security → scroll down → Open Anyway, or
  2. in Terminal: xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/"Event Files.app"

Once per machine. A copy handed over on a flash drive skips all of it — quarantine comes from the browser, so a USB copy just opens.
